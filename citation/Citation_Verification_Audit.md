## Required fixes from the supplied workbook

1. Replace the review's placeholder DOI text with actual DOI identifiers from the reference table.
2. For the correlated-error claim assigned to AutoGen/MetaGPT, retain those works for framework descriptions but use N01 for the failure-mode claim; P10 is marked unsupported/misattributed and P11 partial.
3. Rephrase the Reflexion claim that an incorrect critique makes the next draft “more forceful”; the workbook says this is an inference rather than a result directly tested by the paper.
4. Describe Self-Consistency as sampling multiple reasoning paths and selecting the most consistent answer, rather than as simply adding explicit intermediate steps.
5. Soften the claim that Generative Agents demonstrates error propagation through memory; the workbook says it does not directly study propagation.
6. State clearly that the gap claim attached to citation-generation/FActScore papers is the review author's observation, not a result established by those papers.
7. Use the peer-reviewed venue/year for ReSpAct (IWSDS 2025) and the correct title spelling “Harmonizing.”
8. Verify the precise page/figure claims that the workbook marks as needing a primary-PDF check before submission.
